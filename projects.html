03 / MY APPROACH

How I approach data analysis

One thing I am learning about data science is that working with data involves much more than simply creating a chart. Before analyzing a dataset, it is important to understand what information is available and what questions the data might help answer. For example, when exploring transaction data, I might want to understand which spending categories are most common or how transaction amounts vary across customers.

After identifying a question, the next step is preparing the dataset. This may involve checking for missing values, removing duplicates, reviewing column types, and investigating unusual observations. I have been practicing these steps through Python programming and data processing exercises.

Once the data is ready, visualization becomes an important part of the analysis. I enjoy learning how bar charts, line graphs, and other visual tools can make trends easier to understand. Through my coursework and projects, I want to continue improving my ability to explain findings clearly and create visualizations that are both useful and accessible.

My analysis checklist

  • Identify the problem or research question.
  • Understand and prepare the dataset.
  • Check for missing or inconsistent values.
  • Explore patterns with Python and visualizations.
  • Communicate findings and limitations clearly.
