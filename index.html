01 / ABOUT

Curiosity is where every project starts.

Welcome to my data science portfolio. I am a student interested in the ways technology, mathematics, and creative problem-solving can help people understand the world around them. Data can look like a collection of rows and columns at first, but every dataset contains questions worth exploring. I enjoy learning how to turn raw information into patterns, visualizations, and useful explanations. This website is a place to share that process as my technical and analytical skills continue to grow.

My current learning focuses on programming, exploratory data analysis, and data visualization. I am practicing how to ask clear questions, organize information carefully, and choose charts that make comparisons easier to understand. I am also discovering that good analysis requires more than producing a graph: it involves checking assumptions, communicating uncertainty, and thinking about the people represented by the numbers. I want my work to be understandable to someone seeing a dataset for the first time.

One reason I enjoy data science is that it connects different interests. The same skills can be applied to finance, education, public services, business decisions, and everyday activities. Over time, I hope to build projects that are both technically sound and genuinely helpful. For now, this portfolio documents my progress and gives me space to experiment. Visit the projects page to see a sample interactive chart built with D3.js and read more about the steps involved in a data analysis project.
